CONTROL ENGINEERING · ROBUSTNESS

Vehicle Cruise Control | PI & Anti-Windup

Speed tracking under hills, actuator saturation and parameter changes

PI controlAnti-windupRobustnessMATLAB
FIGURE FROM SUPPLIED PROJECT MATERIAL
01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What the project addresses

Modelled a vehicle longitudinal plant, designed PI speed control, and explored road-grade disturbances, actuation limits, anti-windup and sensitivity functions.

02 / ENGINEERING METHOD

Method and implementation

  • Implemented the nonlinear vehicle model and PI control simulations.
  • Introduced a road-grade disturbance and compared saturated controller behavior with and without anti-windup.
  • Assessed parameter sensitivity and gang-of-four frequency-domain plots.
03 / RESULTS

Evidence and observations

  • Report comparison shows ~0.02 m/s peak overshoot with anti-windup versus ~0.885 m/s without it for one disturbance case.
04 / CRITICAL REVIEW

Scope and limitations

Results are specific to simulated parameters and disturbance setup.
