01 / PROJECT BRIEF

Engineering problem

Can active steering regulation and state estimation remain useful when lateral vehicle dynamics are uncertain or noisy?

Extended the BMW 730 steering model into controller and state-estimator comparisons. Investigated PI pole placement, Luenberger observer design and a Wiener-filter-inspired observer under uncertainty and noise.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My role and technical ownership

AUTHOR CONTRIBUTION

I developed steering-controller and observer comparisons, including PI feedback, Luenberger observation and covariance-weighted estimation.

03 / METHODS & DESIGN CHOICES

How I approached the work

  • Built a closed-loop yaw-rate/steering formulation from the selected vehicle model.
  • Derived PI-controller parameters with characteristic-equation objectives.
  • Compared deterministic observer behavior with Wiener/Kalman-like covariance-weighted estimation assumptions.
