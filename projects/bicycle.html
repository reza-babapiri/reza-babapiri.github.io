01 / PROJECT BRIEF

Engineering problem

Why does balancing performance change with forward speed, and what type of feedback can regulate bicycle roll?

Examined simplified roll dynamics and a Whipple-model bicycle representation, exploring why stability and steering authority change with forward speed.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My role and technical ownership

AUTHOR CONTRIBUTION

I modelled bicycle roll/steering dynamics, tested stability assumptions and studied candidate feedback controllers in MATLAB.

03 / METHODS & DESIGN CHOICES

How I approached the work

  • Used simplified roll equations and a Whipple-type bicycle model to study a balance-control problem.
  • Inspected poles, zeros and steering-to-roll dynamics at different speeds.
  • Compared the behaviour of elementary proportional control with more structured feedback.
