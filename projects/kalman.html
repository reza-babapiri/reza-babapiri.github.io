01 / PROJECT BRIEF

Engineering problem

How can a noisy moving-target trajectory be estimated using a simple state-space motion model?

Built a constant-velocity state-estimation model for a football penalty kick, then studied how a Kalman filter reduces position error under noisy measurements and uncertain initial velocities.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My role and technical ownership

AUTHOR CONTRIBUTION

I implemented the simulated penalty-kick tracking model, Kalman estimator, numerical experiments and plotted evaluation.

03 / METHODS & DESIGN CHOICES

How I approached the work

  • Selected a constant-velocity state representation with explicit process and observation uncertainty.
  • Propagated state predictions and corrected them with noisy measured positions.
  • Compared estimated and reference trajectories across ten simulated initial-condition variants.

06 / CRITICAL REVIEW

Limitations and engineering learning

Simulation-only academic task; not a real camera-based ball tracking system.

Engineering takeaway: This is a stochastic-estimation simulation, not camera tracking or field-tested real-time sensing. Model and noise assumptions determine filter quality.
