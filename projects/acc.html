01 / PROJECT BRIEF

Engineering problem

How should following-distance feedback be organized when lead-vehicle acceleration and disturbances are not directly measured?

Studied adaptive cruise control as a following-distance regulation problem. Implemented and compared a PID approach against an observer-based feedback strategy to estimate unmeasured dynamics and disturbances.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My role and technical ownership

AUTHOR CONTRIBUTION

I developed and compared PID-based and observer-based adaptive cruise control approaches using analytical models and MATLAB simulation.

03 / METHODS & DESIGN CHOICES

How I approached the work

  • Formulated the longitudinal distance-error dynamics with leader behaviour and disturbances.
  • Designed a conventional PID controller as an accessible reference strategy.
  • Evaluated a state-observer-based control formulation and compared tracking and robustness behavior.
