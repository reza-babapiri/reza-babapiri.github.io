POWER ELECTRONICS · SIMULINK

Buck Converter | Cascaded PI Control

100 V to 48 V, 1 kW converter design with current and voltage feedback loops

MATLABSimulinkPI controlBode analysis
FIGURE FROM SUPPLIED PROJECT MATERIAL
01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What the project addresses

Designed and documented a closed-loop DC–DC buck converter using an inner current loop and outer voltage loop, with an additional single-loop model for comparison. Component sizing and controller tuning were studied through steady-state, step-response and frequency-domain simulations.

02 / ENGINEERING METHOD

Method and implementation

  • Sized the inductor and output capacitor around ripple requirements; compared single- and cascaded-loop structures.
  • Used MATLAB scripts and Simulink models to inspect gain crossover, phase margin, transient response and load variations.
  • Refined LC component values based on observed simulation behavior rather than relying on nominal sizing alone.
03 / RESULTS

Evidence and observations

  • Nominal: 100 V input, 48 V output, 1 kW, 50 kHz switching.
  • Reported steady-state: 1.1% output-voltage ripple and 9.6% current ripple.
  • Full-load voltage step overshoot: 1.69%; no-load case: approximately 75% overshoot (important limitation).
04 / CRITICAL REVIEW

Scope and limitations

No-load transient response is significantly underdamped in the supplied report. These are reported simulation results, not physical bench-test measurements.

PROJECT RECORD

Supporting evidence

6-page report; single- and dual-loop Simulink models; MATLAB controller scripts; supporting component datasheets.
