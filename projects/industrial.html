01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What the project addresses

During industrial mechanical design work at Enetaf Sane Co. (Jan 2022–Jul 2023), worked on expansion joints, bellows, flanges and piping-related equipment. The focus was translating service requirements into manufacturable CAD designs, engineering calculations, design checks and drawing revisions. This is an anonymised professional experience case study, not a reconstruction of a client project.

02 / ENGINEERING METHOD

Method and implementation

  • Produced preliminary AutoCAD layouts and SolidWorks part, assembly and manufacturing drawings.
  • Used EJMA-based engineering calculations and applicable ASME and project standards in bellows, material and piping-component design checks.
  • Reviewed guide-rod arrangements, bolted connections and flange thickness under operating-pressure and load considerations; used SolidWorks Simulation and ABAQUS for structural assessment.
  • Proposed material and thickness revisions to address corrosion concerns and supported selection of pipe and valve specifications for service conditions.
03 / RESULTS

Evidence and observations

  • Examples of engineering decisions include material/thickness changes for corrosion mitigation and guide-rod quantity or flange thickness adjustments based on loads.
  • Work involved technical document reviews and coordination with fabrication teams.
