Kalman Filtering | Penalty-Kick Tracking

Tracking a moving target from noisy 2-D measurements

Kalman filterState estimationMATLAB
FIGURE FROM SUPPLIED PROJECT MATERIAL
01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What the project addresses

Built a constant-velocity state-estimation model for a football penalty kick, then studied how a Kalman filter reduces position error under noisy measurements and uncertain initial velocities.

02 / ENGINEERING METHOD

Method and implementation

  • Formulated a 2-D motion state and noisy measurement model.
  • Simulated ten variations of the kick initial state and velocity.
  • Evaluated estimation error at the target-reaching instant.
03 / RESULTS

Evidence and observations

  • The supplied report states all ten simulated cases met its 0.25 m error criterion.
04 / CRITICAL REVIEW

Scope and limitations

Simulation-only academic task; not a real camera-based ball tracking system.

PROJECT RECORD

Supporting evidence
