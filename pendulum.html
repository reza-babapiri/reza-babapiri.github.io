STATE-SPACE CONTROL · NONLINEAR SIMULATION

Inverted Pendulum | LQR & LQI

Cart–pendulum stabilization, tracking, saturation and anti-windup

LQRLQIMATLABSimulink
FIGURE FROM SUPPLIED PROJECT MATERIAL
01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What the project addresses

Analysed the upright equilibrium of a cart–pendulum system and compared state-feedback pole placement with linear-quadratic regulator designs. Extended the study to integral tracking, actuator force constraints and nonlinear response.

02 / ENGINEERING METHOD

Method and implementation

  • Derived and tested a state-space model; assessed reachability and open-loop instability.
  • Compared Ackermann pole placement against LQR based on transients and control effort.
  • Examined integral-action reference tracking, nonlinear attraction limits, saturation and anti-windup.
03 / RESULTS

Evidence and observations

  • Demonstrated controller comparisons in report plots and MATLAB scripts.
  • Identified control-effort versus transient-response trade-offs and local validity of the linear model.
04 / CRITICAL REVIEW

Scope and limitations

Academic simulations; the study does not demonstrate a physical stabilized pendulum.
